# Japanese term list

The terms every Japanese draft on zenbujapanese.com uses, as SERP's
[International SEO](https://github.com/serpcompany/serp/blob/main/docs/marketing/international-seo.md)
guide asks: names that stay as they are, and the one translation agreed for each recurring term.
The keyword briefs are in [`../briefs/ja/README.md`](../briefs/ja/README.md). A native reviewer
changes this list first, then the drafts that use a changed term.

## Who the Japanese pages are for

ADR 0007 names it: `/ja/dictionary/` is a Japanese-English dictionary (和英辞典) for Japanese
speakers. Headwords are Japanese and the meanings are JMdict's English, so a Japanese reader
looks up what a word is in English, or types English to find the Japanese. The kana, kanji, JLPT,
and frequency lists also serve people learning Japanese who read Japanese. The iPhone app's own
screens are in English; the Japanese pages say so where a reader would otherwise expect Japanese.

## Style

- です・ます throughout, plain and friendly; no お客様 or 弊社 in product copy.
- Full-width punctuation (、。「」（）) in Japanese text; half-width for numbers and Latin names.
- No space between Japanese and Latin words (iPhone版, JLPT単語), as most Japanese sites write
  them.
- Rewrite for the reader and the keyword rather than translating sentence by sentence.
