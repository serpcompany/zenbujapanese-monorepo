# Keyword brief: the account pages

The Japanese versions of Sign in (`/ja/login/`), Create your account (`/ja/register/`), No
password needed (`/ja/forgot-password/`), and Your account (`/ja/account/`). Every one is
`noindex, nofollow` and in no sitemap, in Japanese as in English, so these pages target no
keyword: a reader reaches them from the site's header, footer, and each other, never from a search
result. The brief only fixes the title, H1, and meta description each page uses, so the tab, the
heading, and the link that leads there say the same thing. The brand comes from the site's title
template (`… | Zenbu Japanese`), and the H1 is the page's title, as in English.

The words are the agreed ones in [`../../terms/ja.md`](../../terms/ja.md): ログイン and ログアウト
for sign in and out, アカウントを作成 for making an account, and Zenbuアカウント for the account.
The Apple and Google buttons use the wording each company gives for Japanese (Appleでサインイン,
Appleで登録, Appleで続ける; Googleでログイン, Googleで登録, Googleで続ける), so Apple's button says
サインイン where the rest of the site says ログイン. No description names Apple or Google, since a
site offers them only once they're set up. Volumes are not measured, since there are no keyword
targets (see [`README.md`](README.md)).
