# Keyword brief: the company and legal pages

The Japanese versions of About (`/ja/about/`), Support (`/ja/support/`), Contact
(`/ja/contact/`), Sources (`/ja/sources/`), the Legal index (`/ja/legal/`), and the four policies
under it. These pages rank for the brand: a reader who already knows Zenbu Japanese searches its
name with サポート, 問い合わせ, プライバシーポリシー, or 利用規約. So each title names the page with
the word Japanese sites use for it, and the brand comes from the site's title template
(`… | Zenbu Japanese`). The H1 is the page's title, as in English.

The policies are full translations of the English ones, in plain です・ます and the defined terms
Japanese policies use (当社 for "we", 本サービス for "the Service"). Names, the company, its postal
address, email addresses, licence names, and U.S. statutes (17 U.S.C. § 512, 16 CFR Part 255)
stay as they are. Volumes are not measured: no keyword database was used (see
[`README.md`](README.md)).

## For the native review

- Whether the policies should say which language prevails if the English and Japanese versions
  differ. Neither version says so now; that is the owners' decision, not the translation's.
- 当社 and 本サービス as the defined terms for "we" and "the Service", and 施行日：{date} for the
  "Effective" line.
- The DMCA page translates "physical or electronic signature", "under penalty of perjury", and
  "any judicial district where Zenbu Japanese may be found" as 自筆または電子的な署名,
  偽証罪の制裁のもとで, and 所在しうるいずれかの司法管轄区.
- The Terms keep "Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike" and "Licensed Application End User
  License Agreement" in English beside Japanese text, as licence names stay as they are; Apple's
  Japanese name for the second (ライセンス対象アプリケーションエンドユーザ使用許諾契約) is given
  with it.
- The Privacy Policy names the app's tabs and modes with the term list's words (翻訳, プレイヤー,
  画像検索) and calls Listening リスニング; the app's own screens show the English names.
- The Privacy Policy keeps the app's Settings labels in English, in 「」 (Send crash reports,
  Report a Problem…, Included with your report), since the app's screens are English, and calls a
  crash report クラッシュレポート and a problem report 問題の報告.
- Whether support answers in Japanese. The Support and Contact pages don't say either way.
