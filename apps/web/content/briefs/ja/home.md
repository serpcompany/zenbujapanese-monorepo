# Keyword brief: the homepage, `/ja/`

The Japanese homepage sells the same iPhone app as `/`, to a Japanese reader: a Japanese-English
dictionary (和英辞典) that works offline, with a camera reader, handwriting, and a conversation
translator, and a free web dictionary under it. The app's own screens are in English, so the
page says so beside the first screenshots, and the drawings of the app stay English.

Volumes are not measured: no keyword database was used (see [`README.md`](README.md)).

| Role | Keyword | Volume | Why |

| Secondary | 漢字 手書き 検索 | Not measured | Handwriting finds a kanji the reader can't read |
| Secondary | 和英辞典 無料 | Not measured | The web dictionary is free |

The title leads with the brand, as the English one does, and carries the primary keyword
(和英辞典), オフライン, iPhone, and 翻訳アプリ in about 30 full-width characters. The H1 is the
promise rather than the keyword, so it stays short enough for two lines on a phone; the badge
right above it carries the primary keyword. The meta description repeats the keyword and names
each part of the app with the searchers' words (カメラ, メニュー, 看板, 手書き, 漢字, 会話, 無料).

## Sections

- **Hero.** 和英辞典で調べる opens `/ja/dictionary/`. The fine print says the app works offline and
  that its screens are in English.
- **Try the dictionary.** 和英辞典を使ってみる: the same search box as `/ja/dictionary/`, and the
  same four example searches as `/`. The English one, to persevere, shows that English finds the
  Japanese.
- **Area showcase.** 辞書, 画像検索, 翻訳, プレイヤー, and リスト, the term list's names for the app's
  tabs, each with a pitch and four points written for a Japanese reader.
- **Free on the web.** ウェブ版は無料 and the free tools, with the Tools menu's Japanese names.
- **Page end.** 辞書も翻訳も、ひとつのアプリで。

## For the native review

- Whether 和英辞典 アプリ or 英語 辞書 アプリ is the stronger primary keyword for this app, and
  whether Japanese searchers expect an English-interface app under either.
- The H1: whether a keyword H1 (for example オフラインで使える和英辞典アプリ) ranks better than the
  promise.
- The drawing of the website's search page takes its row titles, part of speech, and 英語の意味
  from the same translations as `/ja/dictionary/search/`, so a change there changes it too.
