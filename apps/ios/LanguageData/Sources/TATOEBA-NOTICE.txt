Tatoeba example-sentence attribution

Zenbu Japanese includes modified Japanese-English example-sentence data from
Tatoeba's official 2026-08-08 weekly exports.

Source and downloads: https://tatoeba.org/en/downloads
CC BY 2.0 FR: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/
CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Zenbu's modifications select the lowest-ID directly linked English translation
for each Japanese sentence, normalize the data into an app-owned SQLite schema,
and add deterministic search indexes. Both Tatoeba sentence IDs, contributor
usernames supplied by the official detailed exports, explicit not-supplied
status where those exports contain no username, and the license class of each
side are retained in the bundled database. Named contributors are listed in
Dictionary Sources > Tatoeba > Contributor credits.

Tatoeba and its contributors do not endorse Zenbu Japanese.
