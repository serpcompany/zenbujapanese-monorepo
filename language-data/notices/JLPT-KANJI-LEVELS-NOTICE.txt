JLPT Kanji Levels

Unofficial level estimates from Jonathan Waller's JLPT kanji lists (tanos.co.uk).
License: CC BY
Project: https://web.archive.org/web/20200806005029/http://www.tanos.co.uk/jlpt/jlpt5/kanji/

KanjiReferenceData.json's wallerJlptLevel holds each kanji's level from these lists. Waller's
site licenses what he doesn't sell under Creative Commons BY, asking for credit and a link. It no
longer resolves, so the link is the Internet Archive's copy.
