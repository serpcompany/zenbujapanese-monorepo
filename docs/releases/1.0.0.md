## Locked feature list

1. Search Japanese, English, and romaji.
2. Offer source-backed Japanese readings for exact romaji forms, including
   `iru → いる`, `sushi → すし`, and `taberu → たべる`.
3. Show ranked dictionary results, readings, meanings, parts of speech,
   conjugations, pronunciation, related words, and example sentences.
4. Show kanji meanings, readings, components, classifications, related words,
   and animated stroke order.
5. Recognize handwritten kanji.
6. Recognize selectable Japanese from the camera, photo library, and imported
   files, then offer dictionary navigation and Apple system translation. A
   word opened from Image Text retains its source as Encounter Media. One word
   can retain several images, one image can support several words, and the
   learner can add another image from Word Detail, remove one association, or
   browse and delete retained images in Media Library.
7. Store private word notes and recent searches on the device.
8. Provide in-app privacy, support, dictionary-source, and attribution pages.
9. Align furigana to kanji-bearing spans in mixed kanji/kana words across Search,
   Word Detail, Kanji, Image Text, and linked example words instead of repeating
   the complete reading above visible kana.

## Explicitly outside version 1.0

- Accounts, cloud sync, advertising, analytics, subscriptions, and in-app
  purchases.
- Clippings and flashcards.
- Exact result-count or result-order parity with the retired Nihongo reference
  app where no provider-independent general rule exists.
- Runtime third-party morphology engines; the researched sentence-association
  proof of concept remains deferred.
